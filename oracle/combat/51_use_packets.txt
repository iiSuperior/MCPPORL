# Right-click paths that do nothing: A holds use with a sword while looking at B
# (entity interaction, then the main-hand use, every 4 ticks) and B raises its
# shield while looking at the ground (use on block first). Neither changes the
# fight: A's click lands, B's shield blocks the second one.
start A item=golden_sword
start B z=3.0 yaw=180 offhand=shield
20 idle | idle
9  use | use pitch=70
1  tap | use pitch=0
6  use | use
1  tap | use
10 idle | use
